## What Kind of Game Is A Game About Needle In A Haystack?

A Game About Needle In A Haystack is a single-player, first-person incremental game from Little Field Studios, released on Steam for Windows on October 16, 2026. Store listings and SteamDB tags place it in the simulation, automation, and incremental categories, alongside farming sim and resource management labels. The premise is exactly what the title suggests: you work through enormous quantities of hay, upgrading your tools along the way until manual labor turns into something closer to automation.

According to the historical Steam store copy, the core loop is simple: dig hay, carry it, and sell it — with upgrades steadily removing the friction from every step.

## The Core Gameplay Loop

The verified mechanics describe a straightforward economy built around one resource:

- **Dig hay by hand.** Early on, gathering is entirely manual.
- **Carry it in bags.** Your bag capacity limits how much hay you can haul per trip.
- **Sell hay at a cow.** The cow serves as your point of sale, turning collected hay into income.

This is a classic incremental setup: your early earnings come from slow, repetitive effort, and each sale funds upgrades that make the next round of gathering faster and more efficient.

## Workshop Upgrades Drive Incremental Progression

The workshop is the backbone of progression in A Game About Needle In A Haystack. Verified store copy confirms that workshop upgrades affect three key areas:

- **Bag capacity** — haul more hay per trip, cutting down on back-and-forth time.
- **Hand speed** — dig faster so each gathering session yields more.
- **Tools** — unlock better equipment that changes how you collect hay altogether.

As with most incremental games, these upgrades compound. Bigger bags mean fewer trips, faster hands mean fuller bags, and better tools eventually replace hand digging entirely. Each purchase shortens the loop that made the previous tier feel slow, which is the fundamental appeal of the genre.

## Tools: Pitchforks, Dynamite, and the Vacuum

The store description names three distinct tools that mark clear progression milestones:

- **Pitchfork** — strikes take larger clumps of hay than your hands can, boosting yield per action.
- **Dynamite** — clears pits, removing obstacles that would otherwise block access to hay.
- **Vacuum** — continuously collects hay without manual digging, though it can overheat, adding a light management wrinkle to your automation.

The vacuum is the closest thing to true automation in the verified kit: it keeps working while you do other things, but the overheat mechanic means you can't just leave it running forever.
