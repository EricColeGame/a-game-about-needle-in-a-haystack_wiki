## How the Gameplay Works

Based on the historical Steam store copy, this is a first-person, hands-on hay-collecting loop — think incremental progression wrapped in a farm-sim skin. The core loop is deliberately simple and physical:

- **Dig hay by hand** and carry it in bags
- **Sell hay at a cow**, which serves as the game's point of sale
- **Upgrade at the workshop**, where improvements affect bag capacity, hand speed, and tools

Progression comes from steadily automating and scaling that loop. A **pitchfork** lets you strike for larger clumps of hay per swing, **dynamite** clears pits, and a **vacuum** continuously collects hay — though it can overheat, forcing you to manage it rather than leave it running forever.
